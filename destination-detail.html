Tegalalang Rice Terrace, Ubud

Tegalalang Rice Terrace is one of Ubud's natural tourist icons, renowned for its stunning views of rice terraces. The landscape showcases the beauty of the traditional Balinese irrigation system called Subak, which has been recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Tourists can stroll among the rice paddies, take photos at popular spots like the "Love Bali Swing," or enjoy the rural atmosphere while sipping coffee at a cafe overlooking the expansive greenery.
